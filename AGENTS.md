# Project notes

This is the personal portfolio of Dimas Putra, built with Astro and Tailwind.

## Design work

Read `DESIGN.md` first for the art direction, palette, type, rhythm, and motion dials. Then load the antislop skills before editing UI:

- `antislop` for the core filter and Delivery Gate
- `antislop-ui` for layout, color, components, and motion
- `antislop-layoutmobile` for reflow and mobile behavior
- `antislop-human` for contrast, keyboard, focus, and states
- `antislop-copywriting` for any visible text

The short version: no generic gradients, glass everywhere, pill badges, bento grids, fake statistics, fake testimonials, or decorative icons. Keep the warm paper, ink, vermillion accent, serif display, hairline rules, and the small square motif.
