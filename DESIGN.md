# Dimas Putra portfolio, art direction

Reading this as: a single-page portfolio for product teams and hiring managers, in an editorial specimen-sheet style, dial ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.

## The idea

A printed specimen sheet for a frontend engineer. Warm paper, hard ink, one vermillion accent, hairline rules, and oversized serif type. The page should feel assembled by a person with a red pen, not generated by a template.

The identity motif is the small vermillion square. It marks the current chapter, the primary action, and the live availability line. Nothing else glows.

## Typography

- Display: Instrument Serif. Reason: an editorial voice with real contrast between thick and thin strokes, warm enough to offset the technical content.
- Body: Instrument Sans. Reason: a companion grotesque with tighter apertures than the usual default sans, still quiet at 16 to 18 pixels.
- Annotation: IBM Plex Mono. Reason: chapter numbers, timestamps, and stack lists read as engineering marginalia, not as a terminal costume.

Type scale uses `clamp()`. The hero is 13vw capped at 136px. Body stays between 16 and 18px. Prose is capped at 52ch, about 70 characters per line.

## Composition and rhythm

The page alternates between full-bleed statement, split editorial, inventory table, indexed work list, accordion, and a final split. No two sections share a composition.

1. Header: compact, solid paper, hairline only after scroll.
2. Hero: asymmetric split, masked line reveal, portrait with a stamp, fact strip, then a ticker.
3. Tentang: chapter rule, large serif statement on the left, two columns of prose and facts on the right.
4. Keahlian: an inventory table, four rows that invert on hover.
5. Karya: sticky preview on the left, indexed list on the right, dialog for detail.
6. Pengalaman: sticky statement, four accordion rows.
7. Kontak: oversized serif invitation, copyable email, real form with loading, success, and error states.
8. Footer: one large wordmark, one line of real links.

## Motion

Motion serves orientation, not decoration.

- Hero lines rise out of masks once on load.
- Scroll reveals use four variants: rise, mask, scale, and a drawn rule. Each section uses one, not all at once.
- The portrait tilts a few degrees with the pointer on fine pointers.
- Primary actions are magnetic on fine pointers, capped at 6px.
- Work rows drive the sticky preview on hover or keyboard focus.
- The theme toggle uses a circular View Transition from the button.
- The stack ticker loops slowly and ships with a visible Jeda control. It pauses on hover and focus.
- Accordion panels animate their grid row.
- All non-essential motion is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Copy voice

English, natural and conversational, not stiff or corporate. Short sentences, active voice, first person. No em dashes, no buzzwords, no invented numbers, no fake testimonials. Every number on the page comes from the owner's existing content: seven years, seventeen projects, Lighthouse 95+ at MIFX, and the 2014 to 2019 education range. Placeholders are written as placeholders.

## Decision log

- Warm paper and serif display: the portfolio is a document about craft, so it reads like a printed specimen, not a SaaS dashboard.
- One accent only: vermillion marks the current chapter and the primary action. It is not used for every icon, border, and glow.
- Small radius and hairlines: the grid behaves like a print layout, not a pile of floating cards.
- Sticky work preview: it makes seventeen screenshots scannable without a heavy card grid, and it gives the list a reason to be a list.
- Dialog for project detail: each row is a real control, works on touch, closes with Escape, and shows the full screenshot without inventing a case study.
- Ticker with a pause control: the only loop on the page, and the user can stop it.
- Theme toggle with View Transition: a real transition, not a flash, with an instant fallback.
